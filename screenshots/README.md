## Preserved historical exclusions

Two authentic application viewport captures remain local, named by their grid
dimensions, but their complete v1.0.0 experiment sidecars are unavailable. They
remain ignored and excluded from the public release; hashes are recorded in
[EXCLUDED_MATERIALS.md](../EXCLUDED_MATERIALS.md). Historical v170 illustrations
are indexed in [doc/evidence](../doc/evidence/README.md), not current-release
screenshots. These historical classifications have not been changed.

For every new image record the actual version/commit, source hashes, X/Y/Z,
Start, plane/depth/overlay, mode and parameters, placement/seed, baseline
seed/trials, full profile, observed backend and display scaling/subsampling.
Keep native sidecars where emitted. Label patterns as exploratory observations,
not proofs, and keep diagnostic output out of the public gallery.
